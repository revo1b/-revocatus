Most developers start with the stack. I start with the sales pipeline. Two years running sales operations for a wellness business in Dar es Salaam taught me exactly where businesses lose leads, drop follow-ups, and waste hours on manual work — then I built the software to fix it myself.

Trained originally as a biomedical engineer, I moved into medical equipment and wellness sales, and quietly became the person who builds the internal tools everyone else waits on. Today I design and ship full-stack business platforms, AI WhatsApp agents, and CRM automation — for my own business, and for clients who need software that actually understands how a sales team works.

The result is a rare combination: someone who can sit in a sales meeting, hear the real problem, and walk away and build the system that solves it.
